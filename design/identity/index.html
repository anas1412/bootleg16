What bootleg16 looks like

Each direction is a whole identity: the idea, the wordmark, both faction emblems, colours and what they mean, type, how it talks, and the lobby stage your character stands on. The figures on the stages are stand-ins for your 3D character.

The worldThe 2040s. The Bureau, a private conglomerate, runs the city: "Order is mercy". The Strays, a leaderless network, bomb its pillars: "Nothing left to lose".
The nameA bootleg is something that isn't supposed to exist: a pirated tape, a hijacked signal, an unlicensed copy.
Fixed in every directionAmber is you. Cyan is the Bureau, pink the Strays. The menu is a lobby with your character centre stage.
DIRECTION 1

Channel 16

bootleg16 is a pirate broadcast. Channel 16 cuts into the Bureau's feed and shows what really happens on the streets. The game looks like live TV: a channel bug in the corner, an ON AIR light, lower-thirds with names, the signal breaking up when the picture changes.

DIRECTION 3

Dubbed

bootleg16 is a tape passed hand to hand. The sixteenth copy of a recording the Bureau tried to erase. The look is street print: stencil type, masking-tape labels, paper cards, one sodium streetlight. Rough at the edges, still clean to read.
