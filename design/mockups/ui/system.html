Interference
The UI is a signal. Clean at rest; when something changes it splits into its two channels, Bureau cyan and Strays pink, and tunes back in. The glitch lives only in transitions. Amber is you: your selection, your main action, your row.

Anchor rule

A part on a screen edge mirrors to it: its content and its amber bar sit on the outer side. The bar sits inside the padding, so a row's content lines up with its neighbours'.

Rules

One token source. Colours, sizes and timings live only in client/src/ui/tokens.rs (and tokens.css for the mockup). Screens never define their own.

Glitch only on change. Enter, hover burst, a choice, a value stepping. Nothing stays split at rest.

Over the game: HUD glass tinted 7% to your side, an edge light in your side's colour, TX1/TX2 text only. Menus over the game blur and dim it; the HUD hides.

Red means a problem (can't afford, low health, the last seconds). Waiting or neutral states are grey.

Copy: plain words, no em dashes, labels in caps with letter spacing.

Sounds are made in code (ui/sound.rs): short soft blips, quiet so footsteps stay clear.
